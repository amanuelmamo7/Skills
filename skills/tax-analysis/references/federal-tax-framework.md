# Federal Individual Income Tax — Doctrine Map

The four-stage framework with section and case anchors. Case names are analytical anchors distilled from course study — treat holdings as Tier 2 until read this session; Code sections are read at runtime via the page index (Tier 1 when quoted).

**The arithmetic frame:** gross income − deductions = taxable income; × rate = liability; − withholding/payments/credits = net owed. Policy lenses for evaluating any rule: horizontal equity (like-situated taxed alike), vertical equity (progressivity), revenue effectiveness, coherence with the rest of the system, and complexity cost.

## Stage 1 — Gross income

- **§61 sweep:** all income from whatever source derived. *Glenshaw Glass*: accessions to wealth, clearly realized, complete dominion — the enumerated examples are not exhaustive; punitive damages taxable. Illegal income taxable at FMV (*Collins*).
- **Economic income vs. the Code:** consumption plus change in stored rights; source-irrelevant. The system's deliberate gap: **imputed income** (self-performed services; rent-free use of one's own property) goes untaxed — regressive in effect.
- **Property dispositions (§1001):** gain/loss = amount realized − adjusted basis. Basis: cost (§1012) plus improvements (§1016(a)(1)); higher basis = lower gain and more depreciation. Amount realized includes money + FMV of property received, **and debt relief**: *Crane* (nonrecourse mortgage in amount realized), *Tufts* (even when debt exceeds FMV). Basis subtracts only for rights actually transferred (*First Northwest Industries*).
- **Discharge of indebtedness:** income (*Kirby Lumber* — repurchase of own debt below par). Limits: contested-liability doctrine (*Zarin* — good-faith disputed debt settled ≠ COD income). Employer's payment of employee's tax = income (*Old Colony Trust*).
- **Exclusions (each an island with elements):**
  - Gifts/bequests §102 — *Duberstein*: detached and disinterested generosity, donor's intent controls; moral-obligation payments are not gifts (*Peebles*). Basis: carryover from donor (§1015, with FMV cap for loss); stepped-up to FMV at death (§1014 — the hold-until-death incentive). Consideration-based transfer ≠ gift (*Farid-Es-Sultaneh*).
  - Life insurance §101(a) (transfer-for-value limit, §101(a)(2)).
  - Fringe benefits §132 (no-additional-cost service; qualified employee discount — gross-profit % for property, 20% for services; working-condition fringe keyed to §§162/167 deductibility); §119 meals/lodging — convenience of employer + on premises + (lodging) condition of employment (*U.S. Jr. Chamber of Commerce*). Unsolicited samples deducted as donations must be included in income (*Haverly* — symmetry).
  - Prizes/awards **included** (§74; *Turner* valuation at less than retail when non-transferable).
  - Qualified scholarships §117 (tuition/fees/books; room and board not excludable, Reg. §1.117-6(c)).
  - Damages §104(a)(2) — personal **physical** injury/sickness excluded; punitive and standalone emotional distress not; mixed settlements allocated by nature of the claim, looking to the complaint (*Amos*).
  - State/local bond interest §103.
- **Capital gains machinery:** preferential rates §1(h); loss limits §1211(b) ($3,000 against ordinary) with carryovers §1212(b); definitions §1222 (1-year line). Capital asset §1221 — presumption of capital, defeated only by an enumerated exclusion (*Arkansas Best*; *Corn Products* narrowed): inventory/held-primarily-for-sale (dealer factors — *Rice*: purpose, frequency, development effort), business depreciables/realty, self-created IP, receivables. **Ordinary-income substitutes** stay ordinary: lease-cancellation lump sum (*Hort*), sold lottery installments (*Lattera* — already-earned test), but sale of a right that is itself property can be capital (*Long*). Related transactions characterized together across years (*Arrowsmith*). §1231 quasi-capital netting (gains → LTCG; losses → ordinary). Whole-business sales fragment by asset (*Williams v. McGowan*; §1060 allocation). §1245 recaptures depreciation as ordinary.

## Stage 2 — Deductions

- **§162(a) ordinary and necessary, carrying on trade/business.** Ordinary = customary in the industry (*Welch* — paying another's discharged debts not ordinary); necessary = appropriate/helpful (rarely contested). Personal-expense bar §262 (spiritual consultant not deductible, *Fred W. Amend*; pastor's business errands severable, *Trebilcock*). Paying another's obligations deductible only to protect one's own business (*Jenkins* — the Conway Twitty rule: motivation + O&N). Reasonable salary deductible §162(a)(1); disguised dividends are not — independent-investor test (*Exacto Spring*).
- **§212** extends O&N to income-production activity for individuals.
- **Origin of the claim controls** deductibility of litigation costs (*Gilmore* — divorce origin personal though stock at stake; *Kopps* — suit naming the business is business; *Cavanaugh* — personal conduct origin not deductible).
- **Public policy:** §280E denies deductions to controlled-substance businesses; allocation to non-trafficking activities permitted with objective criteria (*CHAMP* square footage) but not when trafficking is the only profit activity (*Olive*).
- **Capital vs. expense:** courts start from capitalization. Separate-and-distinct asset or significant benefit beyond the year → capitalize (*INDOPCO*); long-lived installations capital (*Fall River Gas*); a termination fee paid in the ordinary course, securing no future benefit, is expensable (*Santa Fe Pacific Gold*). Recovery: depreciation §167 on §1011 basis, ACRS method/period/convention §168; deduction allowed even in year of profitable sale (*Fribourg*); no depreciation without determinable useful life (*Harrah's Club*); §197 intangibles (including noncompetes tied to acquisitions) amortize over 15 years regardless of term (*Frontier Chevrolet*).
- **Mixed personal/business:** commuting nondeductible; §162(a)(2) travel requires reasonable + away from home (home = principal place of business) + in pursuit of business (*Flowers*); the sleep-or-rest rule (*Correll*); assignment >1 year (or realistically expected to be) not deductible (Rev. Rul. line); duplicate living costs from business necessity deductible (*Andrews*). Meals only beyond the personal baseline (*Moss* — daily partner lunches personal). Clothing only if unwearable outside work. Education: maintains/improves skills or employer-required → deductible; minimum qualifications or new trade → not, even if never pursued (Reg. §1.162-5; *Coughlin* — CLE-type expenses deductible); no travel-as-education (§274(m)(2)). Home office through §280A(c)(1) (administrative-activities principal place, post-*Soliman* amendment). Interest §163 (personal-interest limits §163(h); below-market loans recharacterized §7872). Losses §165(c) (business / profit transaction / casualty). Bad debts §166 — business fully/partially deductible; nonbusiness only when wholly worthless, as capital loss; dominant motivation distinguishes (*Generes* — protecting salary is nonbusiness).

## Stage 3 — Timing

- Method governs (§446: cash, accrual, combinations; Commissioner override for methods not clearly reflecting income); inclusion year §451. Cash = actual or constructive receipt; accrual = all-events + reasonable accuracy (+ economic performance for deductions).
- **Constructive receipt** = unfettered control (*Hornung* — MVP car not received until possession; *Ames* — no practical way to compel payment, no receipt).
- **Claim of right:** taxable on receipt under claim of right despite later obligation to restore (*North American Oil*); restoration deducted in the year returned (*Lewis*), with §1341 computing the better of deduction-now vs. prior-year recomputation (>$3,000); no §1341 for embezzlers (*McKinney*).
- **Tax benefit rule:** recovery of a previously deducted item is income in the recovery year at current rates (*Alice Phelan Sullivan*), except to the extent the deduction produced no tax benefit (§111). Lessee improvements excluded to lessor (§109) with no basis step (§1019) — deferral until disposition.

## Stage 4 — Who is the taxpayer

- Dominion and control decides (*Arcia* — custodian of another's funds not taxed).
- **Assignment of income:** services taxed to the earner (*Lucas v. Earl* — the fruit/tree metaphor); property income assigned only by assigning the property interest itself (*Blair* — irrevocable interest assignment effective; *Horst* — detached coupons, retained bond, income to donor); royalties follow control (*Meisner*); contingent fees included in client's income (*Banks* — client controls the tree), but court-awarded statutory fees to counsel of a fee-free pro bono client are not the client's income (PLR line).
- **Family:** child's service income is the child's (§73; *Allen* — parent's cut of signing bonus still child's income absent separate services). Joint returns and the marriage penalty (§1(j)(2)); goals of marriage neutrality, single/married fairness, and progressivity cannot all hold at once. Divorce: alimony/property settlements tax-neutral (current regime); §1041 carryover basis makes low-basis transfers a bargaining chip; unmarried-couple settlements can be excludable when paid to keep sole ownership (*Reynolds*). Innocent-spouse relief §6015 (the *Stephenson* factor walk: joint return, no (b)/(c) relief, no fraudulent transfers, no fraudulent intent, liability attributable to the other spouse — equity factors include abuse and financial exclusion).
