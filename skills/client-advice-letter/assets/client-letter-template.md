# Client Advice Letter — DRAFT

`DRAFT — FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW BEFORE SENDING`
Reviewing/signing attorney: <name — REQUIRED before sending> · Based on: <underlying analysis/memo> · Date: <date>

---

<Date>

<Client name>
<Address / email>

**Re: <the matter, in the client's words>**

Dear <name>,

**[Opening — question + short answer]** You asked whether <question, plainly>. The short answer is <answer with its honest qualifier — e.g., "yes, with one important caution," "probably not, though the law here is unsettled">. This letter explains why and lays out your options.

**[Facts as we understand them]** Our advice is based on the following understanding: <the facts the analysis rests on, briefly>. If any of this is wrong or incomplete, please tell us — our answer could change with the facts.

**[What this means for you]** <The analysis in plain business English. No citations unless requested. Define any unavoidable term of art where it first appears. Explain the why in terms of the client's situation. Keep the calibration honest — a hedge stays a hedge.>

**[Your options]**
1. **<Option>** — <what it involves; tradeoffs: cost, time, risk, relationship>
2. **<Option>** — <same>
3. **<Option>** — <same>

**[Next steps]** <Who does what by when. What we need from you (documents, decisions, dates). What we will do once we have it.>

Please call or reply with any questions — we're glad to talk any of this through.

<Closing>

<Attorney name>

*This letter is a confidential attorney-client communication. Please don't forward or share it outside <company/your immediate team> without checking with us first — sharing it more widely could waive the legal protections that keep it confidential.*

<One-paragraph plain-English why. Facts we relied on + invitation to correct. Options in one line each with the key tradeoff. Next step and date.>

<Attorney name>
